# Personal Development Plan

## Goal
Increase strategic leverage by turning current strengths in engineering operations, leadership, and AI adoption into scalable systems, measurable business impact, and advanced analytical capability.

## Current Strengths
- Equipment engineering and order-quality expertise are at an advanced level.
- Executive communication and change leadership are highly mature.
- M365 Copilot productivity and practical AI adoption are already strong.
- The foundation for agent-building, automation, and data analysis is established.

## Priority Development Areas
1. Advanced automation and workflow design
   - Build more sophisticated Power Automate and AI-assisted workflows.
   - Focus on reusable operational playbooks and escalations.
2. Data depth and decision support
   - Increase SQL and dashboarding maturity for backlog and vendor metrics.
   - Turn reporting into actionable operational intelligence.
3. Custom AI and agentization
   - Expand from prompt-based use to reusable Copilot Studio agents and integrated workflows.
4. Strategic business framing
   - Strengthen ROI, KPI, and business-case communication for leadership.

## 12-Month Development Plan

### Quarter 1
- Complete a targeted project to automate a high-frequency reporting workflow.
- Build one backlog or vendor performance dashboard using Power BI or Excel data model logic.
- Draft a reusable escalation playbook for vendor issues.

### Quarter 2
- Develop one or two Copilot Studio agents focused on operational support.
- Improve SQL fluency by creating practical query sets for backlog, vendor, and quality metrics.
- Publish a leadership-ready summary pack for stakeholder updates.

### Quarter 3
- Operationalize a recurring KPI dashboard for backlog, NFA/FA/API accuracy, and vendor performance.
- Integrate multiple tools into a single workflow that reduces manual reporting time.
- Pilot a reusable AI-assisted meeting and action-item system.

### Quarter 4
- Evaluate impact using time saved, cycle-time reductions, and quality improvements.
- Publish a personal playbook for AI adoption in engineering operations.
- Position for broader influence across teams or enablement work.

## Development Actions
- Learn advanced Power BI / DAX concepts for KPI and trend analysis.
- Deepen SQL query structure, joins, and window functions.
- Build at least two repeatable Copilot Studio agents or automation paths.
- Practice translating technical details into business outcomes for leadership.
- Develop a governance checklist for safe and effective AI use.

## Success Metrics
- Reduced manual reporting effort.
- Faster backlog and vendor issue resolution.
- More consistent executive updates with measurable quality metrics.
- Repeatable AI and automation workflows that can scale beyond personal effort.
- Clear evidence of business impact tied to engineering operations.

## Summary
The plan is not to start from zero. It is to convert a strong operational and AI foundation into a higher-impact, more scalable, and strategically visible capability set.
